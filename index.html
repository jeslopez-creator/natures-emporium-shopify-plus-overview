Shopify vs. a grocery-specific platform

A platform your team runs, not one you maintain

A grocery-specific platform may cover more grocery workflows out of the box. The bigger question is who keeps it running, who can change it, and what it costs as you grow. On Shopify Plus, the platform itself is Shopify's job. Your team's job is merchandising and growth.

🛠️

No maintenance or upkeep

Hosting, CDN, Level 1 PCI DSS and SOC 2 compliance, bot protection and automatic platform updates are part of Plus. No servers, patches or version upgrades for your team to plan around.

🎨

Low-code, no-code site changes

Your team edits pages in the theme editor with drag-and-drop sections and blocks. No code needed for a new banner, landing page or holiday catering page.

🥕

Rich product content in house

Nutrition, ingredients, allergens and certifications live in structured metafields that feed product pages and dietary filters, managed by your team, not code.

⏱️

Schedule it, don't babysit it

Launchpad (Plus only) schedules sales, theme changes and promotions to go live and end on their own. Shopify Flow automates routine tasks, such as triggering delivery dispatch when an order is ready.

🛒

The best-converting checkout

Shopify's checkout converts 15% better on average, and up to 36% better than competitors. Shop Pay can lift conversion by up to 50% compared with guest checkout.¹

🤖

Found in AI search

Through Shopify Catalog, your product data feeds Google AI Mode and Gemini, which reach Canadian shoppers today. The same clean product data powers your site search and filters.

3
Your team, in control

Change the site yourselves, no coding required

Your storefront is built from sections and blocks that your team edits in the theme editor and previews before publishing. Your partner builds the theme once; your team runs it day to day, and Shopify keeps the platform underneath it up to date.

Theme editor

Edit any page, preview, publish

Add, reorder and edit sections on any page, check desktop and mobile, then publish. Weekly specials, a holiday catering page or a new banner become content changes, not projects.

Sidekick

Generate a new block from a prompt

Describe the block you need and Sidekick, Shopify's AI assistant, generates it in the theme editor, ready for your team to customize and place anywhere on the site.

🧱

Built once by your partner

Sections are reusable modules. Once a section exists, it can be used on any page, and each copy shows its own content.

🧑‍🌾

Run by your team

Merchandising, flyer pages, recipes and catering content are arranged and updated by the people who know the products.

🔄

Kept current by Shopify

Hosting, security and platform updates happen in the background, so your three-person IT team isn't planning upgrade projects.
